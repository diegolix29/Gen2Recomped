GEN1 TRUE 3D CHARACTERS - EYE UV FIX TEST1

Target: Gen1Recomp 0.2.53 with Legendary Battle Art.

The previous build reserved a 1024x1024 atlas region for every eye texture.
Six NPC archetypes actually use 512x512 eye sheets, so their UVs sampled twice
the intended width and height. This could display multiple blinking-expression
rows on the face or place facial features toward the sides of the head.

TEST1 contracts the eye UVs for adult_woman, cook, elderly_woman, fat,
staff_man, and staff_woman in their standing meshes and all 24 walking poses.
The other thirteen generic archetypes, Nurse Joy, Professor Oak, player,
Pokemon, model scales, animation, and Battle Art integration are unchanged.

Disable v1.1.5 before installing this test. Fully restart the game.
