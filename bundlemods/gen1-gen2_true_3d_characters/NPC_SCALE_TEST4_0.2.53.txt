This build replaces the removed debug/upvalue attachment with Battle Art's
exported character renderer provider. All original meshes, textures, pools and
walk frames remain inside this NPC mod.

TEST3 identifies each NPC from the live actor supplied by Battle Art. Generic
NPC sprite sheets are no longer replaced with blue marker images. If a model
cannot be created, the ordinary 2D character appears as the safe fallback.

TEST4 also publishes its complete renderer spec. Battle Art TEST12 can discover
and attach it during live rendering if the entry-time registration did not
survive an in-process launcher reload.
