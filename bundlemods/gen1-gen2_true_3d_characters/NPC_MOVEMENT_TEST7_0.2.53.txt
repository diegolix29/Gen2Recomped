GEN1 TRUE 3D CHARACTERS - NATURAL MOVEMENT TEST7

Target: Gen1Recomp 0.2.53 with Legendary Battle Art 1.10.3.

ROOT CAUSE

Battle Art 1.10.3 stores the live NPC direction in ctx.pose.facing. The prior
3D NPC provider read only ctx.facing, which can be nil. A missing value made
the model use its default south-facing rotation even while a trainer was
scripted to approach from another direction.

TEST7 FIX

- Recovers facing from ctx.pose.facing and then the actor as fallbacks.
- Verifies moving direction from real per-frame X/Y displacement.
- Accumulates absolute travel distance so every 24-frame walk cycle advances
  forward; left/up movement no longer reverses the animation timeline.
- Uses a calmer 40-world-pixel gait cycle instead of 32 pixels.
- Rejects large teleport/map-transition deltas so they cannot spin or advance
  a character unexpectedly.
- Applies to every generic NPC and Professor Oak. Nurse Joy receives the same
  direction recovery while retaining her dedicated 60-frame animation.

TEST6's unified NPC skin and the full eye-atlas UV correction remain included.

Disable TEST6 or any earlier version before installing. Fully restart.
