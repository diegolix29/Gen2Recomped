Action Replay v0.1.0

- Initial FireRed-only release.
- Adds CHEATS to the FireRed START menu.
- Ports Walk Through Walls, No Wild Battles, 100% Catch Rate, Infinite Rare
  Candies, All Badges, and One-Hit KO from the original GameShark design.
- Adds a FireRed-native Spawn Pokemon menu containing all 1025 National Dex
  species supplied by FireRed Complete Dex, plus Lv.1-100 selection.
- Uses FireRed internal species IDs when starting the battle, preventing the
  Bidoof/Lickilicky identity mismatch.
