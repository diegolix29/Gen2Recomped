Action Replay v0.1.0 for FireRed Recomp

REQUIRES
FireRed Complete Dex v1.1.0 or newer and Gen1Recomp v0.2.63 or newer.
This is a self-contained mod. It does not replace launcher, runtime, or ROM files.

INSTALL
Import this ZIP through the mod manager after installing and enabling FireRed
Complete Dex. Fully restart Gen1Recomp, load FireRed, press START, then CHEATS.
Disable the old GameShark mod while using Action Replay.

CHEATS
WALK THROUGH WALLS - ignores tiles and NPC collision but keeps map bounds.
NO WILD BATTLES - suppresses ordinary random encounter rolls.
100% CATCH RATE - every legally thrown Ball catches with four shakes.
INFINITE RARE CANDIES - keeps 999 Rare Candies in the FireRed bag.
ALL BADGES - grants all eight Kanto badges and their field-move permissions.
ONE-HIT KO - damaging moves used by the player's Pokemon defeat their target.
SPAWN POKEMON - select any National Dex species #001-#1025, choose Lv.1-100,
and begin an immediate wild battle. LEFT/RIGHT jump 10 entries and L/R jump 50.

Item and badge cheats write real values into the active save. Turning them off
stops replenishment but does not remove items or badges already granted.

Based on the behavior and hook design of GameShark Compatibility v0.7.10 by
goofwear, rebuilt for FireRed's native Gen 3 runtime and menus.
