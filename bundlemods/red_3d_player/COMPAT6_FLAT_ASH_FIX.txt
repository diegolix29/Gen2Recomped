RED 3D PLAYER - COMPAT6 / FLAT ASH FIX

COMPAT5 only removed a battle card when card.tex == textures.player.canvas.
The current BattleScene stack can store the player trainer asset itself (or a
small wrapper around it) instead. COMPAT6 recognizes both representations and
removes only the matching player trainer card before the native battle draw.

The opponent trainer portrait, Pokemon cards/models, UI, weather and NPC paths
are untouched. The existing 3D player injection and shadow path are retained.
