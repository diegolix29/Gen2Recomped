TEST78 / COMPAT9 - WEATHER 4.28.23 TRAINER OWNERSHIP

Built directly from TEST76.

Basis for this test:
- user supplied Weather FX v4.7.1 where the real 3D trainer worked
- user supplied current Weather FX 4.28.23 where the flat backsprite returned
- newer Weather has a substantially expanded battle rendering stack,
  including battle.overlay weather visuals and VoxelScene atmosphere wrapping

TEST78 changes the 3D PLAYER mod, not Weather:
1) Keeps TEST76's real selected 3D trainer injection.
2) Keeps showPlayerBack true so the 3D BattleScene bridge can detect intro.
3) Adds an outer BattleState.draw ownership guard.
4) During ONLY the player trainer-back intro, legacy playerBackPic drawing
   resources are temporarily nil while the full battle draw chain executes.
5) Fields are restored immediately afterward.
6) Guard is reasserted at mods.loaded, game.ready and battle.started so
   Weather/UI load order cannot put another BattleState.draw wrapper outside it.

This deliberately does NOT disable or downgrade Weather 4.28.23.
Clouds, rain, seasons, fronts, banner UI, battle weather, SFX and atmosphere
remain owned by Weather FX.

Use with:
- CURRENT unmodified Weather FX 4.28.23 clouds10 banner-ui
- Dramatic Shape TEST75 Clean 3D Sendout (or later descendant)
- this TEST78 replacing TEST76

Expected:
The old flat trainer backsprite is gone. The real selected 3D trainer should
remain/inject on the player arena cell during the trainer intro.

If flat trainer disappears but 3D trainer is also absent, that is still a very
useful binary result: sprite ownership is solved and the next patch can focus
only on BattleScene injection under the new Weather wrapper.
