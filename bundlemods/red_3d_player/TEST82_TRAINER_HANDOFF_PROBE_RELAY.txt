TEST82 - TRAINER HANDOFF PROBE RELAY

Replaces TEST81's invisible direct graphics print.

BattleScene.render now publishes its exact trainer gates to:
  _G.RED3D_TRAINER_PROBE
  _G.RED3D_TRAINER_PROBE_CODE

Compact code:
  H = Dramatic Shape trainer handoff reached 3D Player
  A = arena.player exists
  B = overworld/battle player exists
  T = final trainerActive gate is true

Example:
  H1 A0 B1 T0
means handoff is good, the player object exists, but arena.player is missing,
which prevents trainer injection.

Use together with Dramatic Shape TEST86, which displays this data from the
known-visible battle.overlay path.
