RED 3D PLAYER - COMPAT7 / BACK SPRITES FLAT ASH FIX

Root cause identified from Dramatic Shape's own battle code:
- When BACK SPRITES is active, OverworldBattle.textures() intentionally does
  NOT create a player BattleScene card.
- BattleState.drawPicsLayer instead preserves the player's trainer back as a
  flat GB-layer sprite during the staged 3D battle intro.
- Therefore COMPAT5/6 could never remove Flat Ash through monCards().

COMPAT7:
- Detects trainer intro through BattleState.showPlayerBack/playerBackPic even
  when textures.player is absent.
- Suppresses ONLY that flat staged trainer-back draw in drawPicsLayer.
- Injects the selected real 3D player into BattleScene as before.
- Enemy trainer portraits, Pokemon, UI, weather, NPCs and ordinary flat battles
  are left untouched.
