Gen2Recomp

A free, fan-made launcher and engine for playing the Pokémon games you own, on PC and Android.

Gen2Recomp rebuilds games from ROM files you import from your own cartridges. It includes a launcher, mods, save tools, a step-counting Pokéwalker and optional cloud saves.

Cloud saves: if you choose to sign in with Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive, Gen2Recomp stores copies of your save files in your own cloud storage, so you can play on more than one device. It only accesses files it created itself. Nothing is sent to us.

Source and releases: github.com/UNDERdecoded/Gen2Recomped
